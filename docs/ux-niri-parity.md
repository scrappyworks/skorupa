## Timing and rendering

The profile sets `animations { off }` in `animation.kdl:1–3`. Ship that behavior on first login. A shortcut reaches its final visual state on the next rendered frame. A finger-driven gesture still tracks the finger, the MRU preview still waits 150 ms, and a drag still has to meet its hold threshold. niri sets its animation clock to complete animations instantly, while the MRU opening deadline explicitly uses unadjusted time. Use elapsed time for recognition and sample visual progress for the intended presentation time on both 120 Hz and 180 Hz displays. [Animation clock setup](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/niri.rs#L2638) · [MRU opening deadline](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/ui/mru.rs#L929) · [niri frame timing](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/Development%3A-Animation-Timing.html)

| If animation is enabled | Upstream default |
|---|---|
| Workspace switch or vertical swipe settling | Spring, damping ratio `1.0`, stiffness `1000`, epsilon `0.0001` |
| Overview open/close, horizontal view movement, window move or resize | Spring, `1.0`, `800`, `0.0001` |
| Visible MRU switcher closing | Spring, `1.0`, `800`, `0.001` |
| Screenshot controls appearing | 200 ms, ease-out quadratic |
| Window opening / closing | 150 ms ease-out exponential / 150 ms ease-out quadratic |

A spring has no configured duration in milliseconds. Preserve its initial position and release velocity when settling a gesture. The screenshot’s opening animation fades the controls and selection mask over the already-frozen image. The hot corner itself and the hotkey overlay have no separate transition. ## Hot corners

**Reference and profile.** Upstream defaults to top-left. The profile explicitly enables top-left and top-right in `gestures.kdl:16–20`. Each target is **one logical pixel square** at the corner of the output’s rounded-up logical geometry. Relative and absolute pointer motion test entry. Toggle immediately on entry, once, with no dwell, click or cooldown. Remaining inside does nothing. Leave and re-enter to toggle again. A touchscreen tap in the same square does not trigger the pointer hot corner. [Hit geometry, `src/niri.rs:3490`](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/niri.rs#L3490) · [Entry latch, `src/input/mod.rs:3081`](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/mod.rs#L3081) · [Upstream gesture settings](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/Configuration%3A-Gestures.html)

**Skorupa.** Implement hit testing in the compositor before ordinary bar input. Allow file drag-and-drop and an established window move to enter overview across workspaces. Suppress the corner during resize, spatial pan, screenshot selection, MRU, lock and trusted consent. A pointer grab retaining client focus does not qualify. Preserve niri’s fullscreen exception: a stationary focused fullscreen window rendered above the top layer bypasses the corner hit test. `Mod+O` and the physical trusted reveal must remain reachable under the appropriate input policy. One window and two windows use identical corner geometry. [Grab exceptions](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/mod.rs#L5966) · [Fullscreen hit-test ordering](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/niri.rs#L3842) · [Fullscreen stacking condition](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/monitor.rs#L1658)

## Recent windows and same-application switching

**Opening and ordering.** `Alt+Tab` opens the compositor switcher and advances through mapped windows in descending focus-timestamp order. `Alt+Shift+Tab` reverses. Both directions wrap. An ordinarily focused window is committed to the history after **750 ms**. First focus without an existing timestamp is recorded immediately. Keyboard interaction can commit early, and entering the switcher flushes the pending focus commit. This matters with the profile’s hover focus: crossing several windows on the way to a target should rarely reorder the entire list. If the layout has no focused window, the first forward press selects the most recent entry without skipping it. An empty list opens nothing. [Ordering and wraparound](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/ui/mru.rs#L574) · [Focus timestamp handling](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/niri.rs#L1485) · [Opening and first selection](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/mod.rs#L2685)

**Confirmation and appearance.** A quick press and release switches immediately without flashing the UI. Holding a modifier for 150 ms reveals a horizontal strip of window previews, titles, selection highlight and scope indicator. Release the **last held modifier** to accept. Enter or Space also accepts. Escape cancels without activating the preview. Hover selects a visible preview, and left-click or touch confirms a hit or cancels on a miss. Pointer selection starts only once the UI is visible. The defaults cap previews at `0.5` scale and 480 logical pixels high, also limiting height to half the output height. The highlight has 30 logical pixels of padding. Held repeatable keys use the profile’s 300 ms repeat delay and 30 Hz rate (`input.kdl:13–15`). [Switcher controls and geometry](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/Configuration%3A-Recent-Windows.html) · [Last-modifier handling](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/mod.rs#L667) · [Pointer selection](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/ui/mru.rs#L1029) · [Space and other modal bindings](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/ui/mru.rs#L1829)

**Scope and app identity.** Start with All. `A`, `W` and `O` choose All, Workspace and Output. `S` cycles All → Workspace → Output. Remember the scope across invocations unless the opening bind supplies one. Left/Right advance, Home/End jump to the ends. niri also derives modal actions from configured focus, close and screenshot bindings. `Alt+grave` enables an exact string match on the currently selected toplevel’s Wayland `app_id`; Shift reverses. If the selected window lacks an app ID, ignore the filter. `Alt+Tab` can remove the filter within the same invocation. Ordinary binding precedence matters: the profile’s `Mod+Tab` means *previous workspace* (`keybinds.kdl:122`), even though niri supplies a default MRU alias on that chord. [App ID extraction](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/ui/mru.rs#L233) · [Filter and comparison](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/ui/mru.rs#L738) · [Binding precedence](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/Configuration%3A-Recent-Windows.html#binds)

**Skorupa.** Use one compositor-owned selection model for both shortcuts. All and Output have the same membership on one screen. Workspace can contain a single primary window or an explicit pair. One match remains selected even after repeated advances. Two matches alternate regardless of their workspaces. Closing a candidate removes it from the open list; closing the last candidate cancels. Commit workspace and window focus together so that the bar never reports the new workspace with the old focused window. Keep preview pixels inside the compositor and apply capture policy when rendering the switcher into a stream. `app_id` is adequate for grouping and carries no permission to activate or capture another client. [Removal and close behavior](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/ui/mru.rs#L1077) · [Confirmation and focus](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/niri.rs#L1201) · [Wayland toplevel identity](https://wayland.app/protocols/xdg-shell#xdg_toplevel:request:set_app_id)

## Overview, navigation and dragging

**Enter and leave.** Start in normal view. The profile toggles overview with `Mod+O`, repeat disabled (`keybinds.kdl:164`), either hot corner, or a **four-finger touchpad swipe up**. Swipe down to close. The upstream sample also binds release of `Mod` alone; that is a sample-config choice absent from this profile. In overview, unmodified Escape or Enter closes on the current selection. Left/Right focus columns. Up/Down first try the corresponding window in the column, then the adjacent workspace. Ordinary configured shortcuts remain available. Clicking a window activates it and exits; clicking the body of another workspace selects it and exits. The gutter is a valid insertion target for a drag, distinct from the body of a workspace. [Sample release binding](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/resources/default-config.kdl#L405) · [Overview keys](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/mod.rs#L5991) · [Pointer activation](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/move_grab.rs#L82) · [Insertion hit testing](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/monitor.rs#L1590)

**Zoom.** Show a live scaled view of each workspace. The default scale is `0.5`. The profile changes the backdrop to `#1a1a1a` (`misc.kdl:40–42`). For progress `p`, the scale is `1 − p × (1 − configured_zoom)`, with the configured endpoint clamped to `0.0001…0.75`. The gap is 10% of output height times current scale, rounded on the physical pixel grid. Background and bottom-layer surfaces travel with the workspaces. The top bar and overlay-layer surfaces stay at screen scale. Four-finger travel of 300 unaccelerated gesture units spans the transition. Use physical up/down independent of natural-scroll preference. Rubber-band at the endpoints with stiffness `0.5`, limit `0.05`; on release project velocity, clamp to `[0,1]`, then round. A stationary midpoint is the boundary. There is no overview pinch-zoom control in this path. [Zoom defaults](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/niri-config/src/misc.rs#L122) · [Progress and release](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/mod.rs#L3895) · [Scale formula](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/mod.rs#L5162) · [Workspace gap](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/monitor.rs#L1371) · [Layer behavior](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/Overview.html)

**Mouse and touchpad.** Left-drag a window without `Mod`. The move recognizer distinguishes a click from motion at 8 logical pixels. Tiled-window detachment has a separate 256-pixel threshold measured after dividing movement by overview zoom; at half scale that corresponds to 128 logical pixels of on-screen travel. A floating window can detach immediately once movement starts. Right-drag pans the workspace beneath the pointer horizontally. Plain vertical wheel navigates workspaces with a 50 ms cooldown, and Shift+vertical wheel navigates columns. Two-finger scrolling supplies continuous navigation: the initial nonzero vertical component selects the workspace axis, otherwise the gesture pans horizontally. A zero/zero scroll ends it. [Move recognition](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/move_grab.rs#L180) · [Tiled detachment](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/mod.rs#L4053) · [Right-drag](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/mod.rs#L3325) · [Overview wheel bindings](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/mod.rs#L3697) · [Two-finger axis recognition](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/scroll_swipe_gesture.rs#L27)

**Touchscreen.** In overview, tap a window or workspace body to enter it. One-finger movement reaching 16 logical pixels starts scrolling on the dominant axis, with ties vertical. Holding on a live window for **500 ms** makes a subsequent motion frame eligible for a move. The reference checks timestamps on motion frames. A perfectly stationary contact need not produce feedback precisely at 500 ms. A second finger during a window move toggles floating in niri. Outside overview, plain touch normally goes to the client; `Mod`+touch can move a window, and a horizontal drag on a tiled window can pan. I found no global touchscreen equivalent of the three- or four-finger **touchpad** recognizers in the pin or profile. [Touch grab and hold](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/touch_overview_grab.rs#L73) · [Tap and second contact](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/touch_overview_grab.rs#L166) · [Touch routing](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/mod.rs#L4757) · [Device-specific gesture guide](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/Gestures.html)

**Skorupa.** Show one tile for a single-window workspace and two side by side for an explicit split. Dialogs travel with their parent. Bare Up/Down traverses workspaces because primary windows do not stack vertically. Left/Right stops at the sole window or selects its sibling. Preserve dragging above, below and between cards to create a workspace. For dropping onto an occupied card, the proposed rule is to show a new-workspace insertion hint unless the user has explicitly requested a split. A full two-column card accepts reordering of its own pair but cannot acquire a third primary window. Show the actual destination before release. Keep the existing bar dots touch-accessible and provide a shell overview button for keyboard-detached use. Reserve the trusted reveal gesture for its security function. [Upstream overview drag behavior](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/Overview.html) · [Skorupa compositor and shell scope](https://github.com/scrappyworks/skorupa#parts)

## Workspaces, gestures and edges

**Keyboard and dots.** Preserve `Mod+1…9` for one-based workspace selection, `Mod+Tab` for the previous workspace, `Mod+wheel up/down` for a single step with a 150 ms cooldown, and the `Ctrl` variants of digits and wheel for moving the active column (`keybinds.kdl:86–122`). The profile gives `Mod+PageUp/PageDown` to another command. Outside overview, `Mod+Up/Down` focuses a window within a column, so those keys usually do nothing in Skorupa’s primary layout. Up/down workspace steps saturate at the ends. Numeric selection clamps to the last workspace available; selecting the current index then returns to the previous surviving workspace because the profile enables auto-back-and-forth. Preserve stable workspace IDs separately from their changing display numbers. [Reference resolution](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/niri.rs#L4021) · [Clamping and previous workspace](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/monitor.rs#L988) · [Focus action dispatch](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/mod.rs#L1857)

**Touchpad.** Three fingers navigate. Accumulate unaccelerated deltas until `dx² + dy² ≥ 16²`. Horizontal wins only if `|dx| > |dy|`. The profile enables natural scrolling, so fingers moving up advance down the workspace list. At normal zoom, 300 touchpad units correspond to one workspace. During normal navigation the legal destination is at most one workspace either side of the starting workspace. In overview the legal range spans the list. At zoom `z`, the touchpad divisor is `300 × (1 + (z−1)/2.5)`; direct touch instead tracks the scaled workspace height including its gap. The vertical rubber band uses stiffness `0.5` and limit `0.05/z`. On release, include idle time, estimate velocity over the last 150 ms, project with deceleration `0.997`, clamp to the legal range and round to the nearest index. A short fast flick can commit where a slow drag of equal length returns. [Three-finger input](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/mod.rs#L4454) · [Bounds and gesture scaling](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/monitor.rs#L1864) · [Release calculation](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/monitor.rs#L2051) · [Velocity tracker](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/swipe_tracker.rs#L4)

**Drag edges.** Upstream horizontal edge scroll defaults to **30 logical px / 100 ms / 1,500 logical px/s**. The profile uses **70 / 70 / 2,600**. Vertical workspace edge scroll operates in overview during a drag. Upstream defaults to **50 logical px / 100 ms / 1,500**, the profile to **90 / 70 / 2,200** (`gestures.kdl:3–13`). The vertical speed unit differs: 1,500 corresponds to one workspace height per second at full input speed. Speed increases linearly with penetration. Leaving the zone resets the delay; pushing against the first or last workspace accumulates no movement debt. During a client data drag, holding over the same window or workspace for **750 ms** activates it and closes overview. Scrolling or changing target resets the hold. [Gesture defaults and units](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/Configuration%3A-Gestures.html) · [Vertical edge calculation](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/monitor.rs#L1966) · [Hold-to-activate](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/mod.rs#L2775)

**Skorupa.** Retain an empty workspace at the bottom and retain named workspaces. Remove an unoccupied, unnamed, inactive interior workspace after navigation settles. Open a new independent window on a workspace immediately below the current one; a background launch must not take the user there without activation. Keep vertical physics unchanged. For a horizontal swipe, the proposed two-column rule is to use the same axis recognition and a projected half-workspace threshold to select the adjacent sibling once. At the outer edge, or with a sole window, keep focus in place. This is an intentional simplification of niri’s continuous horizontal viewport and column-specific snap points. Accept it with one-window, two-window and empty-terminal-workspace cases separately. [Workspace cleanup](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/monitor.rs#L664) · [niri horizontal snapping](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/scrolling.rs#L3280) · [Change to edge snapping in the pinned commit](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/commit/02ee0585b9da359cb30aa2a13a6023a6e471cc23)

## Screen recording

**How the owner records.** `Mod+R` toggles the focused monitor. `Mod+Shift+R` toggles an interactive selection: drag a rectangle, click a monitor for the whole output, or Escape to cancel. Either start chord stops an existing recording. `Mod+Ctrl+R` explicitly stops it (`keybinds.kdl:147–150`, `niri-record:106–142`). The script uses `slurp` for selection and niri JSON IPC for focused-output and logical output geometry. A rectangle exactly matching an output selects the monitor path. Other regions use integer coordinates, even dimensions and a 16-pixel minimum. The script offers no individual-window selection. [niri IPC](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/IPC.html) · [slurp upstream](https://github.com/emersion/slurp)

The script launches GPU Screen Recorder with a monitor name or `region`. Its defaults are 60 fps, H.264, `very_high` quality, full color range, cursor included, desktop audio and MP4, with CPU encoding fallback. Microphone, mixed audio and silent modes are configurable. It checks startup after 600 ms. On stop it sends SIGINT, waits up to five seconds, and copies the **saved pathname** as text if the file exists (`niri-record:12–46,51–104`). This script does not request `-w portal`. The recorder documents KMS access for direct monitor capture on the target hardware. The installed binary’s actual backend, environment overrides and achieved frame rate were not verified live. [GPU Screen Recorder upstream](https://git.dec05eba.com/gpu-screen-recorder/about/#amdintelwayland-root-permission)

**What niri exposes.** Applications can request monitor or window sharing through `org.freedesktop.portal.ScreenCast`, `xdg-desktop-portal-gnome` and PipeWire. With the relevant build feature, niri implements the session-bus service `org.gnome.Mutter.ScreenCast` version 5, including session creation, `RecordMonitor`, `RecordWindow`, start/stop and PipeWire stream announcements. Its public implementation does not bind those entry points to a chooser-approved requester. niri explicitly treats unsandboxed host programs as trusted. It also supports Wayland `ext-image-copy-capture-v1` and older `zwlr_screencopy_manager_v1`. Its dynamic cast target is an additional niri facility. None of those interfaces is an MP4 encoder. **Skorupa.** Preserve the chords, cancellation, successful-stop notification, recording format and pathname copy. Route the recorder through portal `CreateSession → SelectSources → Start → OpenPipeWireRemote`. Use the specified xdg-desktop-portal-wlr 0.9 backend with `chooser_type=simple`, a compositor-owned source selection and a fresh consent-renderer instance when consent is required. `Mod+R` may preselect the sole monitor. Each new stream still requires a fresh choice. Bind requester, source, stream and generation; disable restoration; scope Stop to that generation. ## Focus, activation, fullscreen and maximize

**User focus.** Click or tap activates the window under the input. The profile enables `focus-follows-mouse`, with no configured maximum scroll amount and no cursor warp (`input.kdl:23–29`). On a genuine pointer crossing, niri focuses without raising. It suppresses window hover activation in overview and MRU, skips tab indicators, and prevents a window on the outgoing workspace from stealing focus during a transition. In a split, crossing the divider changes keyboard focus. Repainting under an idle pointer after a keyboard switch must not immediately undo the switch. With one primary window the relevant other focus targets are its dialog, shell surfaces and trusted UI. [Hover implementation](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/niri.rs#L7268) · [Workspace transition guard](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/mod.rs#L1531) · [Seat focus ordering](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/niri.rs#L1334)

**Application activation.** niri’s default `xdg_activation_v1` policy treats a token with no serial as urgency-only for an already mapped window. It checks a supplied seat serial against the latest keyboard enter, pointer enter or touch down. A token must be younger than **10 seconds**, and the request consumes it. Window rules can ignore, mark urgent or focus. Initial mapping is a separate policy: a valid token can request focus, and an ordinary tokenless map can receive niri’s “smart” focus. The profile additionally enables `honor-xdg-activation-with-invalid-serial` (`misc.kdl:21–24`). **Skorupa must change both permissive cases.** Require a fresh token tied to real user input for an application-requested focus change, including a newly opened workspace. Allow background mapping and urgency. A click, MRU confirmation or workspace shortcut remains a direct compositor decision. [Token creation and consumption](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/handlers/mod.rs#L734) · [Initial mapping](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/handlers/compositor.rs#L153) · [XDG activation protocol](https://wayland.app/protocols/xdg-activation-v1)

**Shell integration.** In the inspected shell revision `978b9165`, `crates/skorupa/src/services/niri.rs:38,69,117` reads niri’s `EventStream` and requests `FocusWorkspace` by stable ID. `ui_bind/bar.rs:17` wires the dot click. `wayland/launcher.rs:103` opens the Slint launcher on the overlay layer, and `apps.rs:338` launches the selected desktop entry. `wayland/activation.rs:60–94` already obtains a seat/serial/surface-bound token for a notification action. Preserve `Mod+D` and `Mod+A` for launcher and panel (`keybinds.kdl:11–12`). Carry the user-interaction token through launcher activation too. Replace the niri socket dependency with scoped workspace events and selection requests. The shell can draw dots and report a click without receiving MRU thumbnails, arbitrary activation authority or capture access. The public project currently documents the architecture; these shell line references are from the local checkout. [niri IPC contract](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/IPC.html) · [Skorupa process boundaries](https://github.com/scrappyworks/skorupa#parts)

**Three sizing operations.** Full-width column sizing preserves layout gaps, struts and borders. Maximize-to-edges is an xdg-toplevel state: it fills available area, leaves the bar visible and removes compositor gaps and borders. Fullscreen requests the whole output, uses a black backdrop for undersized content and, when focused and stationary, covers floating windows and the top layer. Overlay surfaces can still be above it in niri. The upstream sample binds `Mod+F` to column maximize, `Mod+M` to maximize-to-edges and `Mod+Shift+F` to fullscreen. The profile uses `Mod+F` for fullscreen, `Mod+Ctrl+F` for expansion into available width, and `Mod+M` for a launch action. Its broad `open-maximized true` rule requests a full-width **column** (`rules.kdl:1–5`). [Sizing semantics](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/Fullscreen-and-Maximize.html) · [Client maximize handling](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/handlers/xdg_shell.rs#L473) · [Client fullscreen handling](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/handlers/xdg_shell.rs#L674)

**Skorupa.** A sole ordinary tile fills the work area and keeps the bar. In a split, remember both column identities, order and proportions when a member maximizes or goes fullscreen. Restore the pair on exit, including the previously maximized state when leaving fullscreen. If the sibling closes meanwhile, restore the surviving window to a one-window layout. Keep client configure/acknowledge semantics and allow workspace navigation out of fullscreen. The trusted strip, physical reveal and consent always outrank client fullscreen. A password prompt must never become an ordinary fullscreen, overview or capture surface. ## Hotkey overlay

The profile sets `skip-at-startup` and binds `Mod+Shift+K` (`misc.kdl:26–28`, `keybinds.kdl:9`). Upstream shows the overlay at startup unless suppressed and the sample binds `Mod+Shift+Slash`. Display a centered **Important Hotkeys** panel with actual effective bindings, one row per selected action. niri draws it at 90% opacity with 14 px sans text, 8 px padding and a 4 px border. It collects common navigation actions, explicitly titled bindings and eligible Mod/Super spawn actions. A custom title takes precedence; a null title suppresses an action unless an explicit title wins. The optional `hide-not-bound` removes unbound rows. [Startup decision](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/niri.rs#L2818) · [Formatting and collection](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/ui/hotkey_overlay.rs#L174) · [Panel appearance](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/ui/hotkey_overlay.rs#L23)

Keep it informational, with no timeout or fade. A key press, pointer-button press, wheel, swipe/pinch begin, touch down/motion or tablet tip/button dismisses it. Plain pointer motion and key release leave it visible. The dismissing event undergoes normal dispatch first, so the first shortcut still works. Screen-reader-consumed input is an exception. Skorupa should give the shell a read-only, display-safe description of the effective bindings, refresh it on config reload, and let the compositor own visibility and input ordering. Suppress the panel during trusted prompts. ## Insights

- **The easiest split-layout regression to miss is the wrong destination.** With one window on each of two workspaces, `Alt+Tab` follows window history, `Mod+Tab` follows workspace history, and `Alt+grave` can cross workspaces while staying within one app. Test all three after moving a window or deleting its old workspace. [MRU opening logic](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/mod.rs#L2685) · [Previous workspace by ID](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/layout/monitor.rs#L1016)
- **The most revealing screenshot acceptance case has three observers.** Put a protected ordinary window beside an unprotected one, open the physical screenshot UI during sharing, and compare the local selection, saved crop and stream. Then introduce a trusted prompt. The human-selected crop may include the ordinary protected window; the stream must stay redacted, and no output may contain the trusted prompt. [Interactive export source](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/ui/screenshot_ui.rs#L697) · [Privacy-rule rationale](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/Configuration%3A-Window-Rules.html#block-out-from)
- **Two launch decisions need owner acceptance.** The proposed occupied-card drop creates a workspace unless a split was explicitly requested. The proposed touch-only entry is a shell overview button. Both affect daily interaction on a detached keyboard. Neither follows automatically from niri’s touch recognizer or Skorupa’s two-column limit. [niri touch behavior](https://github.com/niri-wm/niri/blob/02ee0585/src/input/touch_overview_grab.rs#L73) · [niri overview interactions](https://niri-wm.github.io/niri/Overview.html)
- **Region recording is the concrete dependency to track.** A chooser that draws the correct rectangle can still authorize too broad a source. Acceptance requires inspecting the delivered stream dimensions and edge pixels, cancellation before the first frame, and Stop followed immediately by a new generation. The stock 0.9.0 chooser contract does not establish those properties, and I could not verify a completed Skorupa requester-binding or cropped-source implementation. [Tagged xdpw chooser contract](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/emersion/xdg-desktop-portal-wlr/v0.9.0/xdg-desktop-portal-wlr.5.scd) · [Portal stream contract](https://flatpak.github.io/xdg-desktop-portal/docs/doc-org.freedesktop.portal.ScreenCast.html)
- **Verification boundary.** This is a source- and configuration-verified specification. No live input, recording, compositor action or hardware timing test was performed. Effective runtime overrides, physical touch feel, encoder behavior and 120/180 Hz latency remain unmeasured. No files were modified or left outside the repository.
